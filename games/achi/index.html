Game settings · How to play

A minute to learn.

  1. Place your pieces. Take turns placing one piece on an empty point. Each player has four.
  2. Make a line of three. Horizontal, vertical or diagonal: a complete line wins, even while you are still placing.
  3. Then keep moving. Once all eight pieces are down, slide one piece along a line to a neighbouring empty point. No jumping.

Select a piece to reveal its moves, then choose a marked point. A ring shows your selection; a small dot shows a legal destination.

Use Undo to try another idea. Against the computer, it takes you back to your previous turn. New game starts fresh with your current settings.

Keyboard players can use the arrow keys on the board, Enter or Space to select, and Escape to clear a selection.

Own the centre. It connects to every other point, so it gives you more room to respond.

Look for two threats. Build a position that can make a line in two different ways. Your opponent can only block one.
