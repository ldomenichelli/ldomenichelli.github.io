Game settings · How to play

A minute to learn.

  1. Follow the lines. Select a piece, then move to a connected empty point.
  2. Hunt as the jaguar. Jump over an adjacent dog to an empty point beyond it. If another jump is possible, keep capturing.
  3. Close in as the dogs. Dogs move one step and cannot jump. Surround the jaguar so it has no legal moves.

This board uses the capture-all variant: the jaguar wins by capturing all 14 dogs.

Select a piece to reveal its moves, then choose a marked point. A ring shows your selection; a small dot shows a legal destination.

Use Undo to try another idea. Against the computer, it takes you back to your previous turn. New game starts fresh with your current settings.

Keyboard players can use the arrow keys on the board, Enter or Space to select, and Escape to clear a selection.

Jaguar: keep your escape routes open. A jump that leads to another jump is worth looking for.

Dogs: move as a pack. Isolated dogs are easy targets; a connected wall gives the jaguar less room.
