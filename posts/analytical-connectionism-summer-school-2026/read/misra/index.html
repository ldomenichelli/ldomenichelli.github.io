About these Kanishka Misra lecture notes

These notes ask how the structure of a learner’s input shapes linguistic generalization. They use controlled rearing of language models to separate direct examples from indirect cues and test which constructions emerge.

  • Direct and indirect positive evidence
  • Indirect negative evidence and cues in input
  • Controlled rearing of language models
  • Adjective ordering and indirect evidence
  • Dative alternation, development, and generalization
