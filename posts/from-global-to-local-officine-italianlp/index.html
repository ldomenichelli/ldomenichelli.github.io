From Global to Local

Intrinsic Dimensionality as a Lens on Language Model Representation

Lucia Domenichelli · Officine ItaliaNLP · 29 September 2026

On 29 September 2026, I gave the seminar “From Global to Local: Intrinsic Dimensionality as a Lens on Language Model Representation” as part of Officine ItaliaNLP.

Open the seminar slides (PDF) · Download the slides

About the seminar

The talk explores how intrinsic dimensionality can help us study the geometry of language model representations, moving from a global view of representation spaces to a local view of linguistic structure.

  • Representation geometry in language models.
  • Intrinsic dimensionality and its estimators.
  • From global to local: a lens on linguistic structure.
